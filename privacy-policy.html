Privacy Policy

Brand: Darlene Eslaya

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Darlene Eslaya ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with a personal SMS health alert messaging program.

What this program is

This is a personal, non-commercial alert system. The account owner manually sends SMS health alert messages during her own medical episodes (panic attacks) to a small, fixed group of family members and close friends who have personally agreed in advance to receive them. There is no marketing, no advertising, and no bulk messaging.

Information we collect

  • The mobile phone numbers of the small group of family members and friends who have verbally consented to receive alert messages.
  • The content of the alert messages sent, which may include the account owner's first name and, when known, her general location at the time of the alert.

How we use the information

  • Phone numbers are used only to deliver the personal health alert messages described above.
  • Message content is used only so the recipients know the account owner needs help and can call her.
  • Information is not used for marketing or advertising of any kind.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Phone numbers and message content are processed by our SMS service provider (Twilio) solely to deliver the messages. We do not share this information with any other third parties except as required by law.

Opt-in and opt-out

Recipients join this program by giving verbal consent directly to Darlene Eslaya. Recipients may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message, or by telling the account owner directly, after which no further messages will be sent to that number.

Data retention

Phone numbers are kept only while the recipient remains part of the alert group and are removed when the recipient opts out or asks to be removed.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to Darlene Eslaya, the account owner, through the same personal channels used to arrange participation in this alert program.
